# NutriCare Hub - Project Requirements

## Objective
To provide reliable nutrition education, healthy meal guidance, dietary information, and personalized nutrition support through qualified professionals.

## Core Features
- Health & Dietary Intake: Users enter basic health and dietary info.
- Goal Setting: Users identify nutrition goals.
- Personalized Diet Plans: View and manage custom plans.
- Recipe Hub: Search and explore healthy recipes.
- Progress Tracking: Track nutrition and dietary progress.
- Professional Management: Nutritionists create and manage diet plans.
- Consultations: Online booking system for consultations.
- Admin Control: Full platform management (users, nutritionists, plans, recipes, payments).

## Target Screens
1. **Home Page**: Hero with healthy visuals, services overview, and testimonials.
2. **About Us Page**: Mission, vision, and team of dietitians.
3. **User Profile**: Dashboard for personal info and goals.
4. **Nutritionist/Dietitian Profile**: Professional bio, specialties, and booking link.
5. **Nutrition Assessment Page**: Multi-step form for health data entry.
6. **Personalized Diet Plan Page**: Detailed view of the custom plan.
7. **Meal Planner Page**: Interactive calendar/scheduler for meals.
8. **Healthy Recipes Page**: Searchable grid of recipes with filtering.
9. **Food & Nutrition Education Page**: Articles, tips, and dietary info.
10. **Consultation/Booking Page**: Selection of nutritionist and time slots.
11. **Checkout Page**: Secure payment for plans or consultations.
12. **Payment History Page**: User-specific transaction list.
13. **Admin Panel**: Management dashboard for all platform entities.
14. **Sign Up / Sign In**: User and professional onboarding.
15. **Recent Activities & Progress Tracking**: Visual charts and logs of dietary habits.

## Design Aesthetic
- Minimalistic yet Bold.
- Modern, clean, and professional.
- "Nutrition & Dietetics" aesthetic.
- Responsive landscape (desktop) layout.
